About Orion Jets

Pioneering Excellence in Aviation for Indonesia and Beyond.

Our Genesis

Orion Jets was founded with a singular vision: to revolutionize the aircraft acquisition experience in Indonesia. Recognizing the burgeoning potential and unique demands of the Southeast Asian market, we embarked on a mission to bridge the gap between global aircraft availability and local accessibility. Our journey began by meticulously curating data from premier international aircraft marketplaces, adapting and enhancing this information to serve the specific needs of Indonesian clientele.

We are more than just brokers; we are aviation enthusiasts and market experts dedicated to providing transparent, efficient, and personalized service. Our foundation is built on integrity, deep industry knowledge, and an unwavering commitment to our clients' success.

Our Mission & Values

Client-Centricity

To empower our clients by simplifying the complex process of aircraft acquisition, offering tailored solutions and unparalleled support to ensure their ideal aviation goals are met with ease and confidence.

Integrity & Transparency

To conduct every transaction with the utmost integrity, providing clear, honest communication and complete transparency throughout the buying or selling journey.

Innovation & Expertise

To continuously innovate our services and leverage deep market expertise, ensuring our clients benefit from the most advanced search tools and comprehensive global insights.
